# Customer email wording: three options for each

Nothing here is changed in the code. For each automatic email, the current subject and opening line are shown with
three options. Pick one per email (or write your own) and the wording will be updated.

House rules the options follow: warm and human, service-led (a team looking after the customer), no mention of AI,
and little emphasis on ownership. The facts in each email (prices, terms, dates) stay as they are, because the
agreement requires them.
