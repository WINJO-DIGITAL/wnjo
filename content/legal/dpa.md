### 2. Purpose and scope

2.1 This DPA governs WINJO's processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer when providing services under the Main Agreement.

2.2 The Customer is the Controller and determines the purposes and essential means of processing personal data.

2.3 WINJO acts as Processor only to the extent that it processes personal data on the Customer's documented instructions.

2.4 This DPA does not apply to personal data WINJO processes as an independent controller for its own legitimate business purposes, such as managing customer accounts, processing payments, maintaining business records, handling enquiries and complying with legal obligations. WINJO's own privacy notice governs that processing.

2.5 The Parties intend this DPA to satisfy the applicable requirements of Article 28 of the UK GDPR and relevant UK data protection legislation.

---

### 3. Definitions

In this DPA:

- **Data Protection Legislation** means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 where applicable, and any legislation replacing or amending them.
- **Personal Data** means information relating to an identified or identifiable living individual.
- **Personal Data Breach** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data.
- **Processing** has the meaning given to it under applicable Data Protection Legislation.
- **Sub-processor** means another processor engaged by WINJO to process personal data on behalf of the Customer.
- **Data Subject** means an identifiable individual whose personal data is being processed.

Other terms have the meanings given to them under applicable Data Protection Legislation.

### 4. Details of processing

4.1 The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, types of personal data and categories of Data Subjects are described in Schedule 1.

4.2 The processing will continue for the duration of the relevant services under the Main Agreement, subject to the deletion and return provisions in this DPA.

4.3 The actual data processed will depend on the website, services, functionality and instructions agreed with the Customer.

4.4 WINJO will not deliberately request or process special category personal data or criminal offence data unless the Parties have expressly agreed this in writing and appropriate safeguards have been established.

### 5. Customer responsibilities

5.1 The Customer is responsible for ensuring that it has a lawful basis for collecting, using and sharing personal data with WINJO.

5.2 The Customer must provide appropriate privacy information to individuals whose personal data is collected through its website or services.

5.3 The Customer is responsible for ensuring that its website forms, marketing tools, cookies, tracking technologies and other data collection activities comply with applicable law.

5.4 The Customer must ensure that any instructions given to WINJO comply with Data Protection Legislation.

5.5 The Customer must not instruct WINJO to process personal data in a way that would knowingly breach applicable data protection law.

5.6 The Customer is responsible for the accuracy, quality and relevance of the personal data it supplies or makes available to WINJO.

5.7 The Customer must notify WINJO promptly if it becomes aware of any issue affecting the security or lawful processing of personal data handled by WINJO.

---

### 6. WINJO's processing obligations

**6.1 WINJO shall:**

a. Process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including instructions concerning international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law.

b. Inform the Customer if WINJO believes an instruction infringes applicable Data Protection Legislation, unless prohibited by law from doing so.

c. Ensure that anyone authorised to process personal data is subject to an appropriate duty of confidentiality.

d. Take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

e. Assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of the processing and information available to WINJO, with the Customer's data protection obligations.

f. Not sell, rent or use the Customer's personal data for WINJO's own advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

g. Not disclose personal data to third parties except as permitted by this DPA, instructed by the Customer or required by law.

**6.2** WINJO shall not determine its own purposes for processing personal data entrusted to it as Processor.

### 7. Security measures

7.1 WINJO shall implement and maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks presented by the processing.

7.2 Depending on the services and systems involved, these measures may include:

- Access controls and account permissions.
- Password protection and multi-factor authentication where available.
- Secure transmission of data, including encrypted connections where supported.
- Appropriate security settings for website administration and hosting services.
- Keeping software, plugins and supported systems appropriately updated.
- Restricting access to personal data to people who need it to provide the services.
- Secure handling of credentials and access information.
- Appropriate backup and recovery arrangements where provided or configured as part of the service.
- Measures to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure.
- Reviewing security arrangements periodically and when material changes occur.

7.3 WINJO will take reasonable steps to ensure that security measures remain appropriate to the services being provided.

7.4 The Customer acknowledges that no website, hosting environment, software or internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7.5 The Customer is responsible for maintaining the security of its own accounts, devices, passwords and systems, except to the extent that responsibility has expressly been accepted by WINJO.

### 8. Sub-processors and third-party services

8.1 The Customer gives WINJO general written authorisation to engage Sub-processors where reasonably necessary to provide the agreed services, subject to this section.

8.2 Sub-processors may include providers of:

- Website hosting and infrastructure.
- Website platforms, content management systems and databases.
- Website security, backups and monitoring.
- Email delivery and website form processing.
- Analytics and performance tools, where configured.
- Technical support and maintenance services.

8.3 WINJO shall maintain an up-to-date list of Sub-processors used to process the Customer's personal data. The relevant providers and processing locations must be recorded in Schedule 2 or otherwise notified to the Customer in writing.

8.4 WINJO shall notify the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor that will process the Customer's personal data, giving the Customer a reasonable opportunity to object on data protection grounds.

8.5 If the Customer raises a reasonable objection, the Parties shall discuss an appropriate solution in good faith. If no reasonable solution is available, either Party may terminate the affected service in accordance with the Main Agreement.

8.6 WINJO shall enter into a written agreement with each Sub-processor imposing data protection obligations that provide an equivalent level of protection for the personal data, as required by applicable law.

8.7 WINJO remains responsible to the Customer for the performance of its Sub-processors' data protection obligations to the extent required by law.

8.8 WINJO shall not knowingly authorise a Sub-processor to use the Customer's personal data for its own unrelated purposes.

### 9. International transfers

9.1 WINJO shall not transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom unless the transfer is permitted under applicable Data Protection Legislation.

9.2 Where a Sub-processor or service provider processes personal data outside the United Kingdom, WINJO shall ensure that an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard is in place where required.

9.3 Such safeguards may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism.

9.4 WINJO shall provide reasonable information about relevant international transfers upon the Customer's request.

---

### 10. Assistance with individual rights

10.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, WINJO shall provide reasonable assistance to the Customer where the Customer receives a request from an individual exercising their data protection rights.

10.2 Such requests may include requests for:

- Access to personal data.
- Correction of inaccurate personal data.
- Erasure of personal data.
- Restriction of processing.
- Data portability.
- Objection to processing.

10.3 If WINJO receives a request directly from an individual relating to personal data processed on behalf of the Customer, WINJO shall, where legally permitted, refer the individual to the Customer without responding substantively.

10.4 WINJO shall not independently decide whether a request should be granted unless required by law or expressly instructed by the Customer.

10.5 Any substantial assistance requiring work outside the agreed services may be chargeable, provided the Customer is informed of the proposed cost in advance.

---

### 11. Personal data breaches

11.1 WINJO shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting personal data processed on the Customer's behalf.

11.2 The notification shall, where reasonably available, include:

- A description of the nature of the breach.
- The categories and approximate number of affected individuals, where known.
- The categories and approximate number of affected records, where known.
- The likely consequences of the breach, where known.
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects.

11.3 WINJO shall provide reasonable cooperation to help the Customer investigate, contain and address the breach.

11.4 WINJO shall not notify the Information Commissioner's Office or affected individuals on the Customer's behalf unless instructed to do so or legally required.

11.5 The Customer remains responsible for deciding whether notification to the ICO or affected individuals is required and for making those notifications within the applicable legal time limits.

11.6 WINJO shall not make public statements about a breach involving the Customer's personal data without consulting the Customer, unless legally required.

---

### 12. Data protection impact assessments and regulatory assistance

12.1 WINJO shall provide reasonable assistance to the Customer, taking into account the nature of the processing and information available, where the Customer needs to:

- Carry out a Data Protection Impact Assessment.
- Assess the security of processing.
- Consult the ICO or another relevant supervisory authority.
- Respond to regulatory enquiries relating to processing carried out by WINJO.

12.2 Any substantial additional work outside the agreed services may be chargeable, subject to prior agreement.

### 13. Records, information and audits

13.1 WINJO shall make available to the Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and applicable Processor obligations.

13.2 The Customer may request reasonable information about WINJO's processing and security measures.

13.3 Where information alone is insufficient to demonstrate compliance, the Customer may request an audit or inspection, subject to:

- Reasonable prior written notice.
- Normal business hours.
- Appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- No unreasonable disruption to WINJO's business or other customers.
- Appropriate protection of confidential information and the personal data of other customers.

13.4 The Parties shall first consider whether relevant compliance information, policies, independent reports or other documentation can satisfy the request without an on-site inspection.

13.5 Audits shall be limited to matters relevant to the processing carried out for the Customer.

13.6 The Customer shall normally bear its own audit costs. If an audit identifies a material failure by WINJO to comply with this DPA, the Parties shall discuss the reasonable costs of any necessary follow-up audit.

### 14. Retention, return and deletion

14.1 WINJO shall retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the agreed services, comply with the Customer's instructions or meet applicable legal obligations.

14.2 When the relevant services end, the Customer may choose to have personal data:

a. Returned to the Customer in a reasonably accessible format; or
b. Securely deleted by WINJO.

14.3 The Customer must communicate its choice in writing within 30 days after the end of the relevant services. This is the same export window that applies under the Main Agreement.

14.4 WINJO shall not delete personal data before the 30-day window in clause 14.3 has ended, or before the Customer confirms it has a copy of the data it needs, if that is earlier. Unless the Customer has requested return or deletion earlier, WINJO shall take reasonable steps to delete or return personal data within 60 days after the end of the relevant services, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.

14.5 WINJO may retain copies where required by law. Any retained personal data shall remain protected and shall not be processed for other purposes.

14.6 WINJO does not keep separate backup copies of the Customer's website data. Personal data shall be removed from WINJO's live systems in accordance with clauses 14.2 to 14.4.

14.7 The Customer is responsible for downloading or transferring any website content, customer records or other data it needs before the end of the service, unless WINJO has agreed to carry out that work.

14.8 WINJO may charge for additional migration, export or technical work that falls outside the agreed services, provided the cost is agreed in advance.

### 15. Confidentiality

15.1 WINJO shall ensure that anyone authorised to process the Customer's personal data is subject to an appropriate confidentiality obligation.

15.2 WINJO shall not disclose confidential information or personal data to unauthorised persons.

15.3 This obligation shall continue after the termination of the Main Agreement and this DPA.

### 16. Liability

16.1 Each Party remains responsible for complying with its own obligations under applicable Data Protection Legislation.

16.2 Nothing in this DPA excludes or restricts any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

16.3 Any contractual limits or exclusions of liability in the Main Agreement apply to this DPA only to the extent that they are legally effective and consistent with applicable Data Protection Legislation.

16.4 Neither Party shall be responsible for a failure caused by the other Party's unlawful instructions, acts or omissions, except to the extent the first Party is independently responsible under applicable law.

16.5 Nothing in this section removes any direct statutory responsibility or liability either Party may have under Data Protection Legislation.

---

### 17. Term and termination

17.1 This DPA starts when the Parties enter into the Main Agreement or when WINJO first processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, whichever occurs first.

17.2 This DPA continues for as long as WINJO processes personal data on behalf of the Customer.

17.3 If the Main Agreement ends, this DPA shall continue only to the extent necessary to complete the return or deletion of personal data and any other surviving obligations.

17.4 Either Party may terminate the affected processing arrangement if the other Party commits a material breach of this DPA and, where capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within a reasonable period after receiving written notice.

17.5 Termination does not affect any rights or liabilities that have accrued before termination.

---

### 18. General provisions

18.1 This DPA forms part of the Main Agreement and should be read alongside it.

18.2 If there is a conflict between this DPA and the Main Agreement concerning the processing of personal data, this DPA shall take priority.

18.3 Any amendment to this DPA must be agreed in writing by the Parties.

18.4 If any provision is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue to apply.

18.5 This DPA does not create a partnership, employment relationship or joint venture between the Parties.

18.6 This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales shall have jurisdiction, subject to any mandatory rights a consumer may have.

### Schedule 1: Details of processing

The following describes the processing expected when WINJO provides website-related services. The actual processing will depend on the Customer's website and the services agreed.

**A. Subject matter**

The provision of website design, development, hosting, maintenance, technical support, security, backups and related website services.

**B. Duration**

For the duration of the relevant services under the Main Agreement, together with any period reasonably required for the return or deletion of personal data.

**C. Nature and purpose of processing**

Processing may include:

- Hosting and storing website information.
- Enabling website contact forms and enquiries.
- Maintaining website databases.
- Supporting customer account or booking functionality.
- Maintaining and troubleshooting website systems.
- Performing agreed security maintenance.
- Providing technical support and resolving faults.
- Transferring or exporting data at the Customer's instruction.

**D. Categories of Data Subjects**

Depending on the website, these may include:

- Website visitors.
- The Customer's prospective and existing customers.
- The Customer's employees and representatives.
- Individuals submitting contact, enquiry or booking forms.
- Individuals using website account or membership functionality.

**E. Types of personal data**

Depending on the website, these may include:

- Names.
- Email addresses.
- Telephone numbers.
- Postal addresses.
- Enquiry and message content.
- Booking or appointment information.
- Account usernames and authentication-related information.
- Website usage and technical log information.
- IP addresses and device/browser information.
- Other personal data the Customer chooses to collect through its website.

**F. Special category and criminal offence data**

Not intended to be processed as part of the standard services.

If the Customer requires a website to collect or process special category or criminal offence data, the Parties must agree the purpose, lawful basis, safeguards and additional contractual arrangements in writing before that processing begins.

**G. Controller's rights and obligations**

The Customer:

- Determines the purposes and lawful basis for processing.
- Provides documented instructions.
- Ensures individuals receive appropriate privacy information.
- Ensures website data collection complies with applicable law.
- Responds to requests from individuals.
- Decides how long personal data should be retained.
- Determines whether a breach must be reported to the ICO or affected individuals.
